Candy Dash Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

Candy Dash ("the app", "the game") is a casual endless-runner game published by MR. BROWN ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which can use certain device information to show and measure ads.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (coins, unlocked worlds and characters, badges, best distances, daily-gift streak, and sound settings) in a private file on your device. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show full-screen ads and optional rewarded ads. When an ad is requested, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and app details, ad interactions, and diagnostics, in order to deliver, measure and secure ads. This is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

You can control ad personalization on your Android device under Settings > Google > Ads, where you can also reset or delete your advertising ID. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), ads are shown only in line with the choices you make in the consent form.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data. The game does not request any special Android permissions of its own; internet access is used only so that ads can load.

Children

Candy Dash is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will help.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data through the app is Google, as described above. Because we do not hold your data on our own servers, there is nothing for us to retain or delete; progress data is removed when you uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
